## 📌 Overview

**Financial Analyst AI** is an AI-powered financial analysis web application designed to help **finance students, aspiring equity research analysts, investors, and general users** understand complex corporate annual reports more efficiently.

Corporate annual reports can often contain hundreds of pages covering financial statements, business performance, management discussions, risks, subsidiaries, strategic developments, and other important disclosures.

Finding the most important information manually can be time-consuming.

Financial Analyst AI uses **Google Gemini AI** to analyze an uploaded financial report and transform important information into a **structured, interactive, and visually organized financial dashboard**.

The objective is not simply to summarize a PDF, but to help users understand:

- How the company is performing
- What is driving its growth
- What is improving
- What is weakening
- What risks the company faces
- What management is focusing on
- What investors should monitor

The application is designed with a strong focus on **financial analysis, simplicity, usability, and practical learning**.

# 📈 Executive Financial Scorecard

Financial Analyst AI evaluates the company's performance across four broad areas:

### 🚀 Growth Momentum

Looks at the company's revenue, income, operating metrics, customer activity, and business expansion to understand the pace and direction of growth.

### 💰 Profitability Quality

Examines whether business growth is translating into stronger profitability and highlights important changes in expenses, margins, finance costs, and earnings.

### 🏦 Balance Sheet Resilience

Reviews important balance-sheet factors such as assets, liabilities, debt, net worth, capital position, and other relevant financial strength indicators.

### 🎯 Strategic Execution

Looks at whether management's stated strategy is supported by actual business developments such as new products, partnerships, expansion, investments, subsidiaries, or new business initiatives.

This helps users move beyond individual financial numbers and understand the **broader financial and strategic direction of the company**.

# 👀 Analyst Takeaway

The application converts the overall financial analysis into a simplified analytical summary.

It focuses on four key questions:

### What is Improving?

Highlights areas showing positive momentum, such as stronger revenue, improving operations, business expansion, or increasing customer activity.

### What is Weakening?

Identifies areas under pressure, such as declining profit, rising expenses, increasing finance costs, or weaker operating performance.

### What Are the Main Growth Drivers?

Identifies the major factors that could support future business growth based on information available in the report.

### What Should an Investor Watch?

Highlights important financial, operational, strategic, and risk-related factors that may deserve attention in future reporting periods.
