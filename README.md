# 📊 Financial Analyst AI

> **Live Web Application:** [financial-analysis-copilot.streamlit.app](https://financial-analysis-copilot-kg4okfskbz6ne86yft9ypx.streamlit.app/)

**Financial Analyst AI** is a financial analysis web application designed to help finance students, aspiring equity analysts, and everyday investors read and understand 200+ page company annual reports in seconds.

---

## ⚡ What It Does

* **Automated Data Extraction:** Reads dense financial PDFs and extracts 12–18 core balance sheet, revenue, loan book, and profit numbers along with year-over-year (YoY) growth percentages.
* **Executive Scorecard:** Automatically evaluates the business across 4 strategic pillars: *Growth Momentum*, *Profitability Quality*, *Balance Sheet Resilience*, and *Strategic Execution*.
* **Visual Financial Comparisons:** Provides clean side-by-side growth bars comparing past vs. present segment performance.
* **Personalized Portfolio Analysis:** Connects to live stock market data (NSE/BSE) to compute exact P&L, evaluate how fundamentally safe your buying price is based on the company's net worth, and project 5-to-8-year compounding outlooks.
* **Forensic Deep-Dive:** Audits underlying profit margins, debt solvency cushions, and operational efficiency.
* **Grounded AI Copilot:** Lets you chat with the report and ask custom questions in plain English—grounded strictly in the uploaded PDF.
* **One-Click Export:** Download full summary briefs in `.txt` format or Excel-ready structured `.csv` files.

---

## 🎓 How It Helps Finance Beginners

* **Removes Jargon Barriers:** Explains complex terms like *CRAR, NII, Free Cash Flow, and Operating Leverage* in simple, everyday language.
* **Saves Hours of Skimming:** Replaces days of manual PDF reading with structured, instant intelligence.
* **Teaches Practical Valuation:** Helps beginners understand what real equity research analysts look for in an annual report.

---

## 🚀 How to Use the Live App

1. Open the [Live Web Application](https://financial-analysis-copilot-kg4okfskbz6ne86yft9ypx.streamlit.app/).
2. Drag and drop any corporate annual report PDF.
3. The AI will automatically read the document and generate the full financial dashboard.
4. Use the tabs to inspect metrics, visual charts, deep-dive investigations, and your personal portfolio return.
5. Ask any questions about the report in the chat section at the bottom.
